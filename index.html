If you only remember three things

It’s a compass, not a cage.

The Charter doesn’t tell you what to think. It gives you a way to check your bearings when things get messy — especially when there’s harm, power, or profit in the mix.

It’s built for conflict.

Disputes, breakdowns, “Oh shit, what now?” moments — that’s where it does its best work. It’s designed to be argued with, tested, and updated, not worshiped.

It’s meant to outlive us.

Kids, neighbors, future systems — they inherit whatever patterns we leave behind. The Charter is one attempt at leaving something less chaotic than what we were handed.

Want to stress-test it?

The Charter isn’t fragile. Bring your edge cases, your “what about…” questions, your worst stories. That’s where we find out if it holds.
